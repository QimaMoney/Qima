What you own, less what you owe

Zakat, worked out from what you hold

Qima works out zakat over your whole balance sheet, checks it against nisab at today's prices and reminds you when your hawl completes.

Nisab
85 g of gold or 595 g of silver
Hawl
Counted in lunar years, with a reminder
What counts
Holdings, cash, money owed to you and assets held for sale, less debts due

Qima calculates; the choices behind the calculation are yours.

No account. Your data stays on your devices.

There is no Qima server holding your records. This is where everything lives.

Accounts, transactions and holdingsOn your device. With iCloud sync turned on, also in your own iCloud account.
Statements and bank messagesRead on your device. They are never sent anywhere.
Sign-upNone. No Qima account, no login, no email.
Analytics and adsNone from Qima. No analytics, advertising or tracking.
On-device modelOff until you turn it on, and it runs on your device. On Android, Google's ML Kit then reports figures about its own use to Google, never what it read. On Windows and Linux the model is downloaded once.
Price requestsSent to public market-data providers. They ask for prices only and never include your holdings.
BackupsA file you keep where you choose, protected with a password if you want (AES-256).
App lockFace ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or passcode, and an option to hide balances.

On your Mac and PC too

The same app on the desktop: the same total, the same privacy, free.

Mac From the Mac App Store, under the same listing as iPhone. Open in the Mac App Store
Windows An installer for 64-bit Windows. It isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn the first time: choose More info, then Run anyway. Download the Windows installer
Linux A tar.gz for 64-bit (x86-64) Linux with GTK 3, extracted into your home folder. Installation guide Download for Linux
Android APK For Android devices without Google Play. It doesn't update itself; get each new version from the same page. Download the APK

Windows, Linux and the APK come from Qima's latest GitHub release. Look for the files ending in -windows-setup.exe, -linux-x64.tar.gz and .apk; each has a SHA-256 checksum beside it.
